# NextDST 69

NextDST 69 is a curriculum summary web application for the B.Sc. Digital Science and Technology curriculum, revised in 2569, at Mahidol University. This context keeps the curriculum language consistent between the source PDF and the app UI.

## Language

**Curriculum**:
The full Digital Science and Technology degree structure from the 2569 curriculum PDF, including study plan, course groups, credits, and graduation structure.
_Avoid_: document, PDF content

**Study Plan**:
The year-by-year and semester-by-semester course plan shown in section 2.4 of the curriculum PDF.
_Avoid_: roadmap, schedule

**Course**:
A single listed subject with a course code, Thai title, English title when available, credit value, and category.
_Avoid_: class, item

**Course Group**:
A curriculum category such as General Education, Core, Specialized Elective, Professional Experience, Capstone, or Free Elective.
_Avoid_: tag, type

**Specialized Elective Track**:
One of the specialized elective areas: Internet of Things, Data Science, Cybersecurity, or Software Engineering.
_Avoid_: major, minor, concentration

**Source Document**:
The original Google Drive curriculum file linked from the app navigation.
_Avoid_: reference link, external file
