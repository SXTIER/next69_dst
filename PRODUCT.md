# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Incoming DST students at Mahidol University who need to understand the Digital Science and Technology 2569 curriculum before and during their first planning decisions. They are likely comparing study years, checking course groups, and looking for a quick answer without reading the full curriculum PDF.

## Product Purpose

NextDST 69 turns the official DST 2569 curriculum into a compact web tool for choosing a study year, reviewing the study plan, and finding related courses. Success means a new student can quickly see what each year contains, understand credit structure, and locate course details without getting lost in dense source material.

## Brand Personality

Clear, concise, and easy to understand. The interface should feel academic enough to be trusted, but not formal to the point that new students hesitate to use it.

## Anti-references

Avoid difficult UX, hidden navigation, dense PDF-like pages, unclear filters, and interfaces that make students guess where to start. The app should not feel like a generic marketing landing page or a decorative dashboard where visual style gets in the way of course lookup.

## Design Principles

1. Start from the student's next question.
2. Keep the path short from year selection to course detail.
3. Prefer scannable structure over long explanation.
4. Make curriculum terms consistent with the source document.
5. Use motion only to explain state changes and keep the task fast.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, adequate contrast, semantic controls, and reduced motion. Thai and English course text must stay readable at small sizes, and filtering or loading states should remain understandable without relying on animation alone.
